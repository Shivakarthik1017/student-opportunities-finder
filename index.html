Why this exists

Student jobs, internships, scholarships and free courses are scattered across many sites. Deadlines are missed, eligibility is unclear, and expired or paid links are mixed in with free ones.

  • One list, not ten tabs — every opportunity in one place.
  • Deadlines first — sorted soonest first, with a days-left countdown.
  • Eligibility up front — check if you can apply before you click.
  • Free means free — paid exams and fees are flagged, never hidden.
  • Verified links only — every Apply button goes to the official or source page.

Data last checked: 17 November 2026. Not included on purpose: Amazon Future Engineer Scholarship (India) — applications are currently closed and reopen in 2027 — and TCS NQT, which is a paid test (fees from ₹599), not a free application.
